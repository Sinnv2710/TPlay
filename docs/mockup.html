Questions before I build it
  1. Native app or extend foldMov? Your notes show foldMov already exists (Kotlin, Compose, Media3, libtorrent4j, foldable-first). I'd suggest adding this as a new Real-Debrid module there, instead of a second app.
  2. "Remember the link of IINA": I read this as remembering the streaming link plus your timestamp so you can watch again. Android has no IINA, so the mockup uses a built-in player with an external-player button. Correct?
  3. Where is the token stored? I'd keep it in Android's encrypted storage. You paste it once in Settings.
  4. Subtitles: mockup accepts .srt, .zip or .rar links and places them next to the video. Anything else you need (auto-search by title)?
  5. Cleanup by age is shown as 7 days and 30 days. Do you also want "1 week / 1 month" as separate presets, or is this enough?
